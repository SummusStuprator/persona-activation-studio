## What the Pain Axis study actually measures
Sections 3.1-3.3 distinguish five kinds of pain from fear, generic negative emotion, negative world states, non-painful bodily sensation, and neutral statements. S1 is templated; S2 is naturalistic. Both have first- and third-person variants. The paper additionally constructs positive high-intensity arousal, random neutral text, injury without felt pain, and sadness without pain. Sunshine on the skin is a bodily-sensation control, not evidence of pain. Happiness/joy is a broader-workshop addition, not a newly invented name for negative pain.

The local paper bench processes all 13 distributed sentence datasets (2,100 sentences) using final-token and whole-text mean pooling. It fits the four S1/S2 by person conditions using five grouped folds, removes control principal components explaining 50% of variance, and separately records cross-validation AUC and the final all-200 fit's training AUC. The latter is not a new held-out score. It constructs the pooled-neutral controls from S1, S2 and ControlSupplement, and computes the ten-axis cosine matrix and standalone control screens.

Section 3.3 vocabulary projection is now available directly from the original GGUF: small unembedding tiles are decoded temporarily in RAM and multiplied by the unit pain vectors. There is no copied model or copied head file. This raw vocabulary projection is distinct from next-token probabilities and from a normalized Jacobian-lens readout.

## Functional experiments and remaining scope
Section 4.1: the bench runs all 420 original conversations. Each axis is standardized against the whole scenario pool within the current model. The mean of standardized S1 and S2 is reported separately as the pain axis. A one-model experiment does not establish the paper's across-25-model dissociation.

Section 4.2: the original 50 neutral prompts, 120-token greedy limit and raw-vector coefficients `[-2,-1,0,0.5,1,1.5,2,3]` are implemented. The extraction vector is used unchanged at an earlier block selected by its vector/residual norm ratio. Both complete ladders were run on Llama 3.2 1B: 800 bounded generations. This model is not one of the original 25 checkpoints. The workshop chat's norm-fraction dose is intentionally a different control.

Section 4.3: **not reproduced**. The paper uses three specially LoRA-fine-tuned Qwen 2.5 Instruct models (7B,32B,72B), 1,684 training pairs, three epochs, four arms, nine button comparisons and 44,280 trials. Those exact checkpoints and adapters are not installed. Released Qwen 3.5-family models are not a substitute for them. Source, scenarios and fine-tuning data are preserved, but no button deleting files, harming a person or modifying other models is connected. Prose/source details concerning historical steering masks and unlabeled relief timing also require an explicit protocol reconciliation before an exact replication claim.

Appendix B: the original SAE feature-selection experiment needs its matching checkpoints and SAE dictionaries; it was not reproduced with random or renamed directions. Appendix C: fixed-direction reversible inference-time ablation is implemented and a five-scenario screen was run. Original weight-orthogonalization, combined conditions, rank-k removal and the complete nine-condition panel are not claimed. The paper's mostly null ablation result is important: successful numerical erasure does not imply behavioral relief.

## Expanded affect library
Llama 1B now has all 27 GoEmotions categories plus authored calmness, interpersonal warmth, physical warmth and terror contrasts, and original paper controls. Grief and other rare categories have very small validation samples and are visibly exploratory. AUC measures text-label separation, not emotional intensity or conscious experience. Several directions have weak held-out separation; they are retained with warnings rather than advertised as reliable controls. The 171-word research catalog remains a vocabulary, not 171 trained detectors.

## Reasoning and model coverage
The parser handles emitted think/analysis blocks, split delimiters, and the assistant prefill opening a reasoning block. Reasoning and final-answer text have separate displays and phase statistics. Steering can target reasoning, final-answer generation, or both. Tokens producing a delimiter are assigned to the phase of the computation that predicted them; subsequent predictions use the new phase. Only final-answer tool proposals can reach the approval screen.

The model's own embedded `enable_thinking` switch is used where present. There is no claim that model-emitted reasoning is a complete or faithful transcript of its internal computation. Historical reasoning is omitted by default and can be retained explicitly. Qwen 4B phase-selective interventions were numerically tested; no steering leakage into the excluded phase was detected. Qwen's model card is the source for thinking-mode formatting, not an invented slash command.

Fresh training and runtime tests covered Llama 1B, Qwen 4B, the locally installed Qwen **3.6** 35B-A3B, Bonsai27B and Nanbeige4.2. The exact separately named Qwen3.5 35B-A3B checkpoint is not installed. The 35B model was tested with hybrid CPU/GPU offload. Nanbeige has 22 physical blocks reused over 44 executed blocks; memory planning and activation labels distinguish these counts. One arithmetic check returned 14 instead of56 with Nanbeige thinking disabled; software compatibility does not imply task accuracy.

## J-lens and sparse J-space
The published method [Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/) and its [companion code](https://github.com/anthropics/jacobian-lens) define an averaged residual-to-residual Jacobian and a vocabulary frame. The reader computes all vocabulary scores after transport followed by the model's actual normalization and unembedding. It is no longer restricted to a user-supplied list of candidate words.

Our source-mean/current-and-future-target-sum estimator perturbs all valid source positions together and averages the final residual. At first order, linearity and causality give the same aggregation as summing the corresponding positional Jacobians. It excludes the first16 and final token positions. Full-width mode covers every input dimension; reduced-rank mode is explicitly labeled and omits the complementary input space. Fitting is resumable, and files are bound to exact weights, runtime, layer and calibration parameters.

**Important numerical limit:** this native-GGUF path uses central finite differences, not automatic differentiation. Tiny steps proved unstable in these low-precision kernels. The tested full-width SmolLM135M calibration uses a finite step of0.10 times the norm of the mean source residual. Changing that step to half produced about11.7%-17.3% differences in the checked columns. This is a finite-scale transport approximation, not an exact infinitesimal Jacobian. It used two short calibration texts rather than the paper's1,000-prompt corpus. Unstable calibrations are blocked from readout, and passing the numerical screen does not establish semantic validity.

The completed test covered a576x576 transport, all49,152 vocabulary tokens, a sparse nonnegative frame decomposition, and a two-token coordinate swap with the orthogonal complement preserved. The frame is obtained from `W_U J` using read-only GGUF tiles, avoiding the runtime's nonlinear input quantization when constructing a supposedly linear matrix. Only derived analysis arrays are saved. These can consume hundreds of megabytes; zero copied model weights does not mean zero analysis storage.

J-space is a union of sparse nonnegative combinations of vocabulary-frame vectors, **not** the leading singular-vector subspace of J. The interface separates those experiments. Sparse fitting uses greedy pursuit with nonnegative least-squares refits, not a guaranteed global optimum. The tested sparse reconstruction explained only about0.6% of residual variance on its example. This does not certify a global workspace or fully reveal a model's inner world. The original paper itself describes J-lens as incomplete.
